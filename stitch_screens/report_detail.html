notes Description of the Incident

During routine pressure testing on Well Pad Charlie (WPC-04), a secondary flange seal failed, resulting in a sudden and uncontrolled release of high-pressure gas mixed with produced water. The release lasted for approximately 45 seconds before the automated emergency shutdown (ESD) system fully engaged and isolated the wellhead.

Two technicians were within the 10-meter exclusion zone at the time of the release. Both evacuated immediately upon hearing the release and the ESD alarm. Neither technician sustained injuries, but the release volume triggered an environmental reporting threshold.

account_tree Root Cause Analysis

Preliminary investigation indicates a failure in the procurement process regarding seal specifications. The seals installed during the Q3 turnaround were rated for standard operating pressures but not the peak pressures required during the specific testing protocol being executed.

    Direct Cause
    Mechanical failure of the secondary flange seal under test pressure.
  • search
    Systemic Cause
    Incorrect seal specification entered into the inventory management system during procurement.
